## What your followers get
Your exact trade — entry, take-profit, stop-loss — executed in **their own
Hyperliquid account** with one signature. No deposits with anyone. No "DM for
signals". No screenshot-chasing.

## What protects YOUR reputation
1. **They copy your whole plan, not just your entry.** The stop goes in with
   the trade. Followers who can't blow past your risk management can't blame
   you for their blowups.
2. **Everything is disclosed** — including your 50% share of our 4 bps fee,
   printed on the card. Compare that to undisclosed affiliate rebates: your
   audience sees you chose the transparent rail. (This also satisfies
   ASCI-style material-connection disclosure by construction.)
3. **Your calls become verifiable.** Every link is timestamped at creation;
   every execution is an on-chain fill. Your track record stops being
   screenshots and starts being provable. Nobody else in the signal economy
   can offer that.
4. **Custody never touches you.** Followers sign their own trades. If they
   lose money on a trade they chose to copy at a size they chose, that is
   trading — not a custody scandal with your name on it.

50% of the builder fee on every execution through your links — on-chain
verifiable, dashboard-visible, paid weekly in USDC. Economically the same
shape as a CEX affiliate deal (Bitget pays up to 50% of fees), without asking

Nothing. You share links to trades you were posting anyway.
